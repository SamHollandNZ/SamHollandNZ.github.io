## Problem

A modular synthesiser is an analogue electronic instrument made of separate
modules that can be patched together in different ways to make different
sounds. I have been building one since 2018.

## Design

So far I have designed and built seven modules. Five are my own designs and two
are adapted from old magazine articles. Every module has a custom circuit board
that I designed.

## The demo battery box

EnergyBank needed a small "suitcase demo" of its system, and I was tasked with
building a presentable battery box to power it. The box has an internal
battery management system, a fuse, a key-operated output switch, and a Wi-Fi
to CAN bridge so the demo setup can be controlled wirelessly.

## The field test

During the field test of the system on Lake Taupō, I ran it remotely from my
laptop on the support boat.

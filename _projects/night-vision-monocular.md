## Problem

I built this monocular after finding a surplus image intensifier tube on eBay.
Two things made it hard: the tube needs 15.4 kV, and the optics have to be
positioned precisely.

Because a supply that high carries a risk of producing X-rays, I built a
[Geiger counter](../meter-geiger-counter/) first to learn about high voltage
and vacuum tubes.
